YOUR PROJECT INFORMATION

Privacy & photo use

Spray-Net South Charlotte uses your submitted contact and project details to respond to your request and arrange your consultation.

As you type a street address, the address text is sent through our server to public NCOneMap, York County, and Lancaster County address services to suggest nearby matches. Your name, phone, and email are not sent for this lookup. You can also enter the address yourself.

We record saved-project page opens, visit counts, and progress through booking, photo uploads, and visualizations so our team can follow up on your project.

Booking details are stored privately to reserve and confirm your consultation. Download your confirmed appointment to add it to your calendar. Our internal scheduler does not automatically sync with Outlook.

After a confirmed booking, photos you choose to upload are stored privately with your project. Photos selected for a visualization are sent to our configured image provider to produce your requested preview. Avoid uploading people or documents.

We request separate permission before using a project photo in public advertising. Keep your private project link to yourself. For access or removal requests, contact your local Spray-Net representative.
