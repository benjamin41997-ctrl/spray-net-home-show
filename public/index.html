A few things to know.

Do I need a photo at the home show?

No. Submit your project details and request your consultation first. Your private studio unlocks after submission, so you can add photos and explore colors later.

Are the visualizations really free?

When the studio is connected, submitting your project unlocks three successful visualizations at no charge to you. You can submit a lead on its own or also request a consultation. No purchase is required. Our team covers the rendering cost.

Does submitting book an appointment?

Submitting saves your project details. A consultation request asks our team to arrange a time. If an online booking calendar is available, you can choose a time after submitting. An appointment is confirmed only through our team or that calendar.

Will my home look exactly like the image?

A visualization helps you explore an idea. Lighting, screens, surface texture, and AI edits affect the image. Your local team will help you choose using physical samples.

Privacy & photo use

Spray-Net South Charlotte collects the contact and project details you submit so our team can respond to your request and arrange project follow-up.

Photos you submit are stored with your project. Photos selected for a visualization are sent to our configured image-generation provider to produce the image you request. Avoid uploading people, documents, or anything you do not want processed.

Uploading a photo does not authorize its use in public advertising. We request separate permission for that. To request access to or removal of your project information, contact your Spray-Net South Charlotte representative.
